Screens/Headphone controls

Headphone controls: a summary paragraph over two action rows and a conditional toggle. The densest use of `SettingRow` in the app — icon, primary and secondary text, and a trailing switch all in one list.
×

Screens/Status

The connection status report after a run. The one failed row is the interesting one: it swaps the service summary for help text and paints the failure message in `support05`, which is the role with the least contrast to work with in dark.
×

Screens/Up Next history

Up Next history: an explanatory paragraph over dated rows. Plain `TextH40` list rows with no leading icon or trailing control, which is the sparsest list style in the app.
×

Screens/Up Next history details

One saved queue, with the formatted date promoted into the app bar title and a Restore text action beside it. Episode rows carry artwork, a three-line text block and a divider.
×

Screens/Logs

The log viewer: monospaced lines under an app bar that carries copy and share actions. The one screen here whose content is deliberately not styled by the design system.
×

Screens/What's New

The What's New sheet over a scrim: illustration header, headline, body and a full-width confirm button. The only modal in this catalog, and the only screen that dims what is behind it.
×

States/Up Next history — error

The generic error state: a single centred line of `TextH50` under the screen's own app bar. Worth a sticker of its own because it is the shared shape every list screen in this module falls back to.
